Welcome to BOOK SHOP

This is a simple online bookstore website made for the BCS-353 Web Designing Workshop.

Students can browse books related to CSE, ECE, EEE and Civil departments, add books to the shopping cart, and view the total amount.

Website pages

  • Login: Enter a username and password.
  • Registration: Fill in the registration form.
  • Catalogue: View the available books and add them to the cart.
  • Cart: Change quantities or remove books.
